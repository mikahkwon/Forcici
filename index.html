Chocolate Chip Cookies

Easy chocolate cookie recipe

Prep Time: 15 minutes    Cook Time: 10 to 12 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175 Celcius.
  2. In a large bowl, cream together the softened butter, white sugar, and brown sugar.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla extract. Mix until combined.
  4. In a separate bowl, mix together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until a dough forms.
  6. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  7. Scoop the dough onto a baking sheet, leaving space between each cookie.
  8. Bake for 10 to12 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden.
  9. Let the cookies cool before serving.

EAT AND ENJOYYY

Take the cookies out of the oven, let them cool, and enjoy them while they are still soft and warm.
